# Chair prototype status

Implemented for SharedMatch (AI and multiplayer): 60-second elimination timer,
halfway re-chair rules, survivor rescue progress, hunter pickup and chair placement.

Not yet validated on two browsers: pickup / rescue button feedback and animation,
AI rescue routing, carrying pathfinding, seated pose, rocket launch animation.
The standalone single-player simulation is not yet connected to ChairSystem.

Do not treat this prototype as a release-ready chair mechanic.
