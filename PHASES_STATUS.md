# Standard rules implementation status

SharedMatch now supports seven ciphers / five to power, one-shot Borrowed Time,
60-second Detention, two-cipher hatch reveal, last-survivor hatch opening,
stationary downed recovery (12 seconds), balloon struggle (10 seconds),
teammate healing (4 seconds), and no forced five-minute elimination.
Online standard rooms fill missing seats to 1v4. Solo practice remains smaller.

Not release-verified: five browsers playing a full game; multiple decoders and
calibration bonus race conditions; AI multi-floor chair/rescue routing; rocket
launch animation and balloon ropes; randomized safe spawns; disconnect handling.
Stages 1-3 should not be described as fully complete until these checks pass.
